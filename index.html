menu_book Quick Start Guide - How to use Scope

1

Open LM Studio

Launch LM Studio on your machine. (Make sure you have downloaded at least one LLM, like Llama 3.1 or DeepSeek-R1).

2

Start Local Server

Go to the double-arrow tab (<->) in the left sidebar and click "Start Server" on the default port 1234.

3

Upload Documents

Drag and drop your resume file above. Optional: Paste the target job description to match mandatory key skills.

4

Analyze Privately

Click "Critique Resume". The backend will parse your files, run a private analysis, and show your dashboard!

web Dashboard Features & Navigation Guide

history

Interactive Scan History

Toggle the menu (three lines) in the top navbar to slide open your scan history sidebar. Clicking any item instantly reloads past scores and critiques.

content_copy

One-Click Copy & PDF Export

Copy optimized recruiter-approved bullet rewrites directly to your clipboard using the Copy button, or export a print-ready PDF critique report using the Download button.

terminal

Terminal Raw Text & Data Sweep

Click the terminal icon in the top navbar to inspect the raw text extracted from your PDF or images. Click the sweep icon next to it to clear your local session cache.

memory

⚙️ Extracting Text...

Your data stays fully local. This may take 15–30s for the DeepSeek/Llama model to run reasoning steps.
